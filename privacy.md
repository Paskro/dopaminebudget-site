# Privacy Policy — Dopamine Budget

## English version

### 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data the Dopamine Budget app for Android ("App") processes, why, and what choices you have. The version of the App covered here works without an account and without cloud sync. The data controller is Pavel Skrobov, an individual residing at Stefana Musića 20, Novi Sad, Serbia ("we"). Contact: contact@paskro.com. This policy is published at https://dopaminebudget.paskro.com/privacy/ and applies from 26 November 2026.

**In short**

- There are no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud sync. Your habits, points, history, settings and progress are stored only on your device. We do not receive them, and we do not keep them on any server.
- Because we do not receive this data, we cannot see it, recover it, restore it or delete it for you. You control it on your device.
- The App does not connect to our servers and does not use any crash-reporting service.
- There is no advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics and no sale of data.
- The App is for people aged 18 and over. It is not medical advice.

### 2. Data we process

**2.1 Data stored on your device (we do not receive it)**

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Habits: name, emoji, point value | Core function of the App |
| Sessions, habit taps with time and time-zone offset, day statuses, streak data, activity dates, reports | Calculating your balance and reports on the device |
| XP events and level | In-app progress |
| Display name (up to 12 characters), language, vibration preference, notification times, do-not-disturb window | Personalization |

This data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, and the home-screen widget reads it from there. We do not collect it, transmit it or store it on any server of ours. This version of the App has no PIN lock and no encryption of its own: habit names and other data are stored as plain text in the App's local database. They are protected only by the Android app sandbox and by your device lock. Anyone who can unlock your device, or who gets full access to it (for example root access or a forensic copy), may be able to read them. Android Auto Backup is switched off for the App.

**2.2 No crash reporting or analytics**

The App does not use Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics or any other crash-reporting or analytics service. It does not send technical, usage or device data to us or to third parties.

**2.3 Permissions**

The App asks Android for permission to show notifications, to schedule exact alarms (so that reminders arrive on time) and to vibrate. Android also grants the App, without asking you, the permissions to restore reminders after the phone restarts, to keep the device awake briefly while a reminder is processed, to run a short foreground task and to check whether a network is available. The App itself does not use the internet and does not request the internet permission. You can withdraw any permission that Android asks you for in Android settings. The App does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

**2.4 Data we do not collect**

Account or sign-in data, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, payment or financial information, health records. If you write to us, we process your email address and your message only to answer you and to keep a record for legal claims.

**2.5 Google Play**

When you download the App, Google processes installation data under its own privacy policy. We see only aggregated statistics in Google Play Console, such as installs, crashes and ratings. The Data safety section of our Google Play listing is completed to match this policy.

**2.6 Habits can be personal**

Some habits you track may relate to your health or behavior, for example smoking, alcohol or food. The data stays on your device and we do not receive or process it. Habit names are visible on the App's own screens and in screenshots you take. The home-screen widget shows only the emoji of your habits and one number (in the calibration phase, points spent; in the control phase, the remaining balance), and in other states a general text. Notifications contain only general text and never habit names, emoji or balance. Still, choose names and emoji you are comfortable showing to people near you. You are responsible for what you choose to record. If a later version ever stores such data on our servers, we will first publish a new policy and ask for your explicit consent.

### 3. Legal bases, recipients and transfers

We do not process the data stored on your device, because we do not receive it. The limited processing we do carry out rests on these bases (GDPR and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection):

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Answering your emails and keeping a record of them | Legitimate interests; performance of a contract |
| Meeting legal obligations and defending legal claims | Legal obligation; legitimate interests |

We do not take automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

**Recipients**

| Provider | Role | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google LLC and Google Ireland Ltd.: Google Play | App distribution | Installation data (processed by Google under its own policy) |
| Email hosting provider of our domain paskro.com | Receiving your emails | Your email address and message |

Google acts under its own terms: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. We disclose data to others only to run the App, to comply with law, or to protect our rights.

**International transfers**

Google may process Google Play installation data outside Serbia and the EEA, including in the United States, under its own policy. We ourselves do not transfer personal data abroad. Emails you send us are handled by the email hosting provider of our domain, which we choose for its data protection terms and which may process them outside Serbia and the EEA.

<a id="delete-data"></a><a id="account-deletion"></a>

### 4. Retention and deletion

**Data on your device.** It stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it, uninstall the App, or open Android Settings > Apps > Dopamine Budget > Storage > Clear data. Deleting a habit or a past session inside the App only hides it from view: the record stays in the App's local database until you clear the App's data or uninstall it, and XP cannot be deleted inside the App. Clearing the data or uninstalling is immediate and cannot be undone, and we cannot restore it. There is no account, so there is no account to delete, and we hold no habit data about you on our servers.

**Emails.** We keep your emails as long as needed to answer you and to handle legal claims, and then delete them within 3 years after the last message in the conversation.

**If you use more than one device.** Each device keeps its own separate data. We cannot move or merge it.

### 5. Security, your rights, age, medical disclaimer, changes and contact

**Security.** Your data stays in the App's private storage on your device. This version has no PIN lock and no encryption of its own, so it is protected only by the Android app sandbox and by your device lock. Keep your device locked with a screen lock, keep Android and the App updated, and be careful with screenshots, shared devices and what the widget shows on your home screen. No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise that your data cannot be accessed by someone who has access to your unlocked or compromised device. If we ever learn of a data breach that affects data we do hold, such as emails you send us, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

**Your rights.** We hold almost no personal data about you, so most choices are yours on your device (section 4). Because we do not hold the data on your device, requests for access, deletion or portability of it mostly do not apply: you can view and edit it in the App, and you can erase it completely by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. If we cannot fulfil a request for this reason, we will tell you. For the little we do hold, such as emails you send us, depending on where you live (GDPR and UK GDPR, Serbian law, CCPA and CPRA, and others) you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Write to contact@paskro.com; we answer within 30 days. We may need to verify who you are, and we cannot find data that is not linked to you. You can complain to your data protection authority. In Serbia this is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising, and we do not track you across apps or sites.

**Age.** The App is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us and we will explain how to delete the data on their device.

**Not medical advice.** The App is a self-tracking and awareness tool. It is not a medical product and does not replace a doctor or psychologist. We do not recommend using it on your own for severe addictions, such as alcohol or drugs, or for eating disorders. If a habit seriously lowers your quality of life, or managing it alone is hard, please contact a specialist. The App does not monitor you or contact anyone on your behalf: if you are in crisis or at risk of harming yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away.

**Changes.** We may update this policy. The "applies from" date will change, and material changes will be announced in the App or on the Google Play listing. If a later version adds an account, cloud sync or any collection of your habit data, we will publish a new policy first and ask for your explicit consent before it applies to you. We will not start collecting your habit data quietly.

**Contact.** Pavel Skrobov, Stefana Musića 20, Novi Sad, Serbia, contact@paskro.com.

### 4. Хранение и удаление

**Данные на вашем устройстве.** Они остаются на устройстве, пока вы их не удалите. Чтобы удалить их, удалите Приложение или откройте Настройки Android > Приложения > Dopamine Budget > Хранилище > Очистить данные. Удаление привычки или прошлой сессии внутри Приложения только скрывает их: запись остаётся в локальной базе Приложения, пока вы не очистите данные Приложения или не удалите его, а XP внутри Приложения удалить нельзя. **Письма.** Мы храним ваши письма столько, сколько нужно, чтобы ответить вам и обработать юридические претензии, а затем удаляем их в течение 3 лет после последнего сообщения в переписке.
